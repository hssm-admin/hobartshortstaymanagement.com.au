Hobart Short Stay monthly draw: terms

Last updated 29 September 2026

Promoter. Hobart Short Stay Management Pty Ltd (ACN 700 759 191), 6/111 Macquarie Street, Hobart TAS 7000, admin@hobartshortstaymanagement.com.au ("we", "us").

1. The promotion

The Hobart Short Stay monthly draw. Each calendar month is a separate promotion (a "Promotion Month"), starting in October 2026 and running each month until we end it by notice on this page. An entry is only valid for the draw of the Promotion Month in which it is made. Entries do not carry over.

2. Who can enter

There are two ways to enter, and both go into the same draw:

(a) Guests: guests aged 18 or over who stay at a property managed by us, entering through the Hobart Short Stay Club.

(b) Instagram: Australian residents aged 18 or over, entering through our Instagram account.

Our employees and contractors, the owners of properties we manage, and their immediate families cannot enter.

3. How to enter

(a) Guests. During your stay, or within 7 days after you check out, scan the QR code at the property and complete the form at hobartshortstaymanagement.com.au/pages/club with your first name and email address, then tick the box and submit. Entry is free.

Entering this way also joins you to the Hobart Short Stay Club, so you agree to receive our club emails. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, and your entry still stands.

(b) Instagram. During the Promotion Month, follow @hobartshortstaymanagement on Instagram and comment on that month's giveaway post on our account. Tagging a friend is welcome but not required. Entry is free. Entering through Instagram does not join you to the club.

Limit of one entry per person per Promotion Month, whichever way you enter and however many comments you make. An email address that is already a club member cannot enter again through the club.

4. Valid entries

(a) We check each guest entry against our booking records. A guest entry is valid only if it was made using the QR code at a property during, or within 7 days after, a stay at that property.

(b) An Instagram entry is valid only if the comment is made on that month's giveaway post before 11:59pm (Hobart time) on the last day of the Promotion Month, from an account we can see, and the entrant is following @hobartshortstaymanagement at the time of the draw.

We may exclude any entry that is incomplete, made by automated means, offensive, or does not meet these terms.

5. The draw

The draw for each Promotion Month takes place at 11am (Hobart time) on the 1st day of the following month, at 6/111 Macquarie Street, Hobart TAS 7000, by electronic random selection from all valid entries for that month made either way. We draw one winner and up to two reserve entries.

6. The prize

One prize per Promotion Month: a $500 accommodation voucher (value $500 including GST). The total prize value for each Promotion Month is $500 including GST.

The voucher can be put towards one stay at any property managed by us, for any dates, subject to availability. It must be used for a stay that starts within 2 years of the draw. The winner books by contacting us directly. The voucher is applied to the total cost of the booking, including the cleaning fee. If the booking costs more than $500, the winner pays the difference. Any unused value is not refunded. Normal house rules and guest limits apply. Travel, meals and any other costs are not included. The voucher cannot be exchanged, transferred or redeemed for cash.

7. Winner notification

We will contact the winner within 2 business days of the draw: by email for a guest entry, or by direct message from @hobartshortstaymanagement for an Instagram entry. We will never ask a winner for a payment or a password. The winner must reply within 14 days to claim the prize. If the prize is not claimed, we will redraw it at 11am on the next business day after those 14 days, at the same place, from the reserve entries. We will publish the winner's first name (and, for an Instagram entry, their Instagram username) and the Promotion Month at hobartshortstaymanagement.com.au/pages/club and on our Instagram within 7 days of the draw.

8. Privacy

For guest entries we collect your first name, email address, the property you entered from and when you entered, to run the draw and the club, and we check entries against our booking records. For Instagram entries we see your Instagram username and comment, and if you win we ask for your name and email address to arrange the voucher. We handle your information as set out in our privacy policy. We cannot accept an entry without this information.

9. Instagram

This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram or Meta. By entering through Instagram you release Instagram and Meta from any liability in connection with the promotion. Questions about the promotion go to us, not Instagram.

10. General

If the promotion cannot run as planned for reasons beyond our reasonable control, we may change, suspend or end it, subject to any direction from a state or territory regulator. Our decision on the validity of entries and the outcome of the draw is final. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for any loss or damage arising from the promotion or the prize, except for any liability that cannot be excluded by law, including under the Australian Consumer Law. These terms are governed by the laws of Tasmania.
